What this run is. Same recipe as the 7-way model, with Reg S-P and Reg S-ID removed from the label space. Their messages (2,082 + 2,040 training rows) were not discarded: they were relabelled no flag, so the negative class now contains out-of-class samples and the model has to learn that privacy and identity-theft content is not one of the four covered regimes. no_flag is 43.6% of training rows; no reweighting was used.
  • Test macro-F1 0.983 [0.977, 0.989] vs 0.977 for the 7-way model: a small gain, but not a like-for-like one. The 7-way model's own predictions collapsed to the same five classes (S-P/S-ID → no flag) score 0.985, so dropping the two hardest classes is what moved the number, not the retraining.
  • Out-of-class rows (S-P/S-ID source) in the test split are 99.0% correctly “no flag”.
  • Remaining in-distribution errors are the familiar Reg BI → FINRA 2210 (7) and a handful of 17a-3/4 ↔ no-flag swaps.
